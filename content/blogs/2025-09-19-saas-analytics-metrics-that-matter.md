\## Introduction

Analytics are the backbone of any SaaS business. Measuring the right metrics informs decisions and drives growth.

\## 1. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

Tracks predictable revenue each month. Helps forecast growth and identify trends.

\## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Know how much it costs to acquire a new customer. Essential for budgeting and marketing efficiency.

\## 3. Churn Rate

Measures customer retention. A high churn rate signals product or onboarding issues.

\## 4. Lifetime Value (LTV)

Predicts the revenue a customer will generate during their relationship with your SaaS.

\## 5. User Engagement

Monitor logins, feature usage, and activity to understand how customers interact with your product.

\## Conclusion

Track, analyze, and act on these metrics to make smarter business decisions and scale sustainably.
